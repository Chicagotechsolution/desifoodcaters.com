Listing standard

What every listing must state before it's published.

Directories rot when they fill with stale, vague entries. Ours stay useful because a listing can't go live without the four facts families actually decide on — and a listing that stops answering for them comes down.

Placement is never paid. Cuisines and regions sort alphabetically, and there are no "featured" slots to buy.

  • Cuisine, named preciselyHyderabadi is not "North Indian." Listings claim the cuisines they cook, not the ones that search well.
  • Capacity rangeSmallest and largest event the kitchen will take, in guests.
  • Metro and radiusHome base plus how far delivery and on-site service actually travel.
  • Dietary factsPure veg or mixed kitchen, Jain options, halal/zabiha sourcing — stated, not implied.
